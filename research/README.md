# `research/` - claim, ancestry, and falsification

This is joints's research record, not production code. Each dossier separates
three questions engineering prose usually collapses: **what useful thing is
being claimed, what did the world already know, and what evidence could prove
it wrong?**

At the time of writing there is no `src/`. The order is deliberate - the
falsifier for the central claim is measurable before a parser exists, and it is
cheaper to find out in an afternoon than in a year.

| Document | What it holds |
|---|---|
| [`LANDSCAPE.md`](LANDSCAPE.md) | the whole map: what tree-sitter measurably is, the five monoids, what must grow in irregex, the vocabulary, and the order of proof |
| [`joinery/`](joinery) | the one genuinely new claim: [what is ours](joinery/CLAIM.md), [who got here first](joinery/PRIOR_ART.md), [how it dies](joinery/TESTING.md) |

Read the landscape first. It is the argument; the dossier is the part of the
argument that has to be earned rather than cited.

If a claim fails, it gets a `CLOSED.md` next to it rather than a quiet deletion.
A closed route is worth a citation to rediscover instead of a month.
