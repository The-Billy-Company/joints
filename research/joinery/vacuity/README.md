## The one-sentence answer

**Fourteen external-scanner rows were seated today; each one moves exactly the
grammar it was seated for and no other grammar by a single column.** The
collateral claims are now supported rather than asserted. Four clearances in the
record are unsupported and none of them is false.

Two corrections landed on this lane the same day, both from `consort/`:

- **Every arm on this lane read `square 0`** (`consort/RESULT-5-blindness.md`),
  so "no other grammar by a single column" was thirty-one columns of outliner's
  own words about its own forest. Re-taken with an oracle seated in all
  twenty-one arms, **the clearance holds** - 13,728 cells, zero movement, and
  byte-exact parse trees agreeing - which is `consort/RESULT-8-sighted.md`.
- **"Cooperating" does not survive the retake.** Every pair residual on `square`
  is zero or positive, which is a ceiling rather than a coupling; scala's good
  row is one row's worth read twice, not two seatings adding.

## The method, and what it costs

The fifth house rule's control is *today's live tree with your own rows deleted,
plumbing left in.* `outside.zig`'s `troupes` roster is a data table of one row
per grammar mechanism, so that control is mechanical: the rows seated today are
the rows in the working tree and absent from `HEAD`, and deleting one un-seats
its terminals while every line of `marrow.zig`, `caesura.zig`, `fence.zig` and
`offside.zig` stays. Rebuilt, that is an isolation arm by the rule's own
definition.

Two costs, both real:

1. **It is a reconstruction.** These landed hours ago; the arm is taken now. It
   answers *does this seating break another grammar in today's tree*, which is
   the question worth answering, and not *did the lane's own pair prove it*,
   which it did not.
2. **It only reaches roster rows.** A runtime fix that changed no roster row is
   in both arms and invisible to this instrument. Those are sorted in
   `RESULT-1` by their own evidence instead.

## The second lane - the two blind spots, closed

The instrument named below was the brief for a second pass, which also took the
ablation family's other blind spot. Both are landed.

**A board now carries the tree it read.** `standing.py` takes a `still` witness
on every run - the per-file manifest folded to a digest, printed in the footer
and embedded in `--json` - and `--against`/`--twice` compare the two trees before
the numbers, refusing at exit 4 when they differ in a file the lane did not
claim. It costs 24 ms on a 946 ms board. The three controls above are judged by
it today without a rebuild: one file, `src/kernel/quire/gather.zig`, separated
the trees of the two that disagreed - and re-boarding them shows **that file is
not what moved scala**, which the board had no field in which to say either way.
`RESULT-4-witness.md`.

**Single-row ablation mis-sizes two of five pairs.** A row can only move a
grammar whose externals it fully seats, so the fourteen rows admit five two-row
subsets and no three-row subset - five arms, not 16,369. Kotlin's two rows claim
**40 KB of a 20 KB defect** (residual −20,288 B) and scala's interact by +5,500;
julia, elixir and swift are additive. No pair is invisible: every one has a
member that moves its grammar alone, so the failure is mis-attributing size, not
missing the defect. `RESULT-5-pairs.md`.

Those two halves met: the retained union arm `aud-iso` disagreed with the fresh
pair arms by 226-296 bytes on four grammars, and the witness said why - it was
pinned from a tree carrying three of another lane's files. Rebuilt from one
snapshot it is byte-identical, and the conclusion it nearly overturned stands.

## Two corrections carried in from `consort`

Both of these narrow results above; neither was known when they were written.

**Every arm in this family was blind to the oracle.** All fourteen singles and
all five pairs read `square 0 · crooked 0 · graded —`, because the private
`OUTLINER_WORK` that makes an arm an arm is where the oracle's verdicts live.
`square` is the only column that is a claim about agreement with a second
parser, so **"fourteen rows, no collateral" was measured on `damage` alone** and
a change can leave `built` untouched while moving every leaf to a different
parent. 28 of the 33 boards on this disk are blind the same way. It is closed at
the root now - `pin.py oracle <pin>` mints an arm's own verdicts, `pin.py arm`
says on stderr when an arm has none, and `standing.py --against` exits 4 rather
than print a delta between two silences. `consort/RESULT-5-blindness.md`.

**Scala's pair in `RESULT-5-pairs.md` was subtracted from a poisoned control.**
The two solo arms were right - 11,913 and 6,557 re-derive to the byte - but the
control carried a since-withdrawn 12,733-byte press regression, exactly the one
`RESULT-3-press.md` handed back. Clean, both worths are positive (+7,763 and
+2,407) and the +5,500 residual is −7,372. Read with the oracle it is not a
coupling at all: each row alone costs 100.0% and 97.0% of scala's entire
`square`, and two rows cannot each destroy all of a quantity and also sum. The
one-sentence answer above should read *scala's good row is two seatings whose
constructs overlap*, witnessed now by a 42-byte specimen rather than by a
residual. `consort/RESULT-6-scala.md` · `consort/RESULT-7-witnesses.md`.

## The instrument I trust least

**`standing.py`'s board, on a single reading.** Not because a column is wrong -
930 numbers came back identical across three separate processes, twice, hours
apart, and its `--twice` is honestly built (separate processes, so the folio
decisions and the oracle memo are re-taken rather than inherited). It passes its
own stability check completely, and that check does not clear it.

`--twice` asks *is this binary's board reproducible*. It cannot ask *is this
board's tree the tree I think it is*, and on a tree ten agents edit those are
different questions. Two of my own controls, four minutes apart, were each
perfectly stable and disagreed by 63 standing points on scala. A third, ten
minutes later, agreed with the first. Every one of those three boards would have
been reported as solid by its own instrument, and any single one of them, quoted
alone, would have been a different claim about the corpus.

So the board's failure mode is not noise, it is **confident timestamping of a
moment presented as a property of a change**. The repair is not a better board;
it is `pin.py`'s per-file manifest, which is what let me say the two controls
differed in exactly two files and name them - and it is why the isolation family
had to be re-anchored to one snapshot rather than measured against a live tree
that was correct every time I read it and different every time I looked away.
