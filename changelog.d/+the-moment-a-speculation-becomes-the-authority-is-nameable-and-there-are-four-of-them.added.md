A `sided` orphan is a reading spared on authored associativity whose warrant is
refuted by the very token that spared it - a hypothesis with a one-token
warrant, the same shape `unwind` found in a supply. The open question is why one
is fatal and another load-bearing when the cell, the class and the authored side
are identical, and the candidate answer was: whether anything later *confirms*
it, which is runtime evidence no table cell can carry.

This is the instrument for asking, and it is behaviour-neutral - the corpus is
byte-identical (verilog damage 62852, built 51.6%). A `sided` cast records the
work item that cast it; a refuted move marks itself dead; a cast whose caster
died this same token mints an identity. Keyed on identity and not on offset,
because haskell's 128 supplies produce 255 zero-width nodes and an offset join
double-counts there even where verilog's is exact. Under `OUTLINER_TRACE=quire`
the parse names each moment every surviving reading descends from one orphan -
the moment a speculation becomes the sole authority for the scanner's slate.

It localises exactly. On the `parameter-port-list.v` witness it fires once, at
byte 125, on the comma - the split that was root-caused by hand. Over
picorv32.v it fires eight times from four orphans, at 43874, 71921, 76287 and
88641, in a 94 KB file.

One of those four is worth the whole instrument. Orphan #3 takes sole authority
at **76287**, and the 441-byte `statement` that an earlier repair attempt shredded
is at **[78013,78454)** - 1,726 bytes downstream. That repair was judged to have
*relocated* the defect. It did not: there is no kill at 78013, and the loss
there is cascade from a kill at 76287. The correction matters more than the
instrument, because "relocated" and "cascaded" argue for different repairs.

The confirmation count itself is **not** delivered, and the reason is the
finding. The frontier can only be asked inside the forking path, because the
moment a fork collapses the orphan is written back as an ordinary reading and
stops being distinguishable from one the table chose. Moving the question after
the token silences it entirely. So the identity dissolves precisely where the
confirmation would have to be observed - which is itself the strongest evidence
yet that the discriminator is not in the table: it is not anywhere durable at
all. Whether that dissolution is the defect or merely hides it is open.
