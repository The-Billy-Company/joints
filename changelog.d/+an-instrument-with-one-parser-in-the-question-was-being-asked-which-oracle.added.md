The blind axis assumes every measured figure is a comparison against a second
parser, and some instruments compare a thing to itself. `shear.py` presses the
same bytes with the same grammar twice; *which oracle* has no answer for it, and
the classifier had no way to say so, so it refused the changelog fragment
announcing that fix within the hour of it being written.

`instrument.py` is the spelling, and it is read off the instrument rather than
off a list of names. For each module a page cites it parses the record
declarations with `budge.declared` - the same reader the field sweep uses - and
asks four questions:

| question | how it is answered |
|---|---|
| what does it report? | every `_` segment of every declared field, so `cut_rubble` and `whole_rubble` both report `rubble` |
| can it be asked an oracle? | does any declared field carry the oracle's vocabulary |
| does it reach one? | does it import `differential`, the one module here that spawns tree-sitter |
| does it have two arms? | do its fields come in qualified pairs over a shared stem - `whole_roots`/`cut_roots`, `one_first`/`many_first`, `cold_read`/`mean_read` |

Paired arms, no oracle column, no reach to the driver, and the instrument is
**self-comparing**; a figure in one of its columns is exempt. Nothing here is a
name, so an instrument written next month is classified by the shape of its own
record and not by whether anybody remembered to add it. On this tree that reads
five - `amend`, `order`, `probe`, `repair`, `shear` - against 39 that can be
asked an oracle, and `differential` itself lands on the right side of the line,
which is the assertion most worth watching fail.

Adjudication is **per column**, not per page: a page cites several instruments
and quotes several columns, and it is exempt only when every column it put a
number beside is reported *exclusively* by self-comparing ones. A column
`standing` also reports earns nothing no matter what else the page names, and a
column nothing on the page reports at all earns nothing either.

**It exempts one page today, and that is the finding.** Of 111 blind pages, 19
name no instrument, 60 cite code reporting none of their columns, 31 quote a
column an oracle-capable instrument also reports, and 1 is self-comparing. The
gap was real and is closed; it was never what makes the blind axis wrong. See
`research/joinery/consort/RESULT-12-refusal.md` for what is.
